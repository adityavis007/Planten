## 1. Overview
At **Planten**, we respect your privacy and provide you with full control over your personal account and associated data. This document outlines how users can request the deletion of their Planten account and all associated personal and agronomic data in accordance with Google Play's Data Safety and Account Deletion policies.

---

## 2. Steps to Request Account & Data Deletion
Users can easily request permanent deletion of their account and all associated data at any time, even if the app has been uninstalled from their mobile device.

### Method: Email Deletion Request
1. Send an email to **adityavishakarma4@gmail.com** from the email address registered with your Planten account (or specify your registered Google Sign-In email).
2. Use the subject line: `Planten - Account Deletion Request`.
3. In the body of the email, please include:
   - Your Full Name (as entered during onboarding).
   - Your Registered Email Address.
   - (Optional) Reason for account deletion.

Our team will verify your request and process the deletion. You will receive an email confirmation once the deletion is initiated and completed.

---

## 3. Data That Will Be Permanently Deleted
Upon receiving and confirming your account deletion request, the following information will be **permanently and irreversibly removed** from our active databases and cloud storage:

- **Authentication Data:** Your Firebase Authentication account, unique user identifier (UID), and email/OAuth authentication tokens.
- **Farmer Profile Details:** Your name, village, district, state, and registered primary crop choices.
- **Scan & Diagnosis Records:** All crop scan records, symptom inputs, AI diagnosis histories, and local/cloud synchronization metadata.
- **Cloud-Backed Photos:** Any leaf or crop disease images uploaded to cloud storage (if cloud photo backup was enabled).

---

## 4. Data That May Be Retained
- **Non-identifiable Agronomic Logs:** Highly aggregated, anonymized disease trends (e.g., total count of disease occurrences by crop type without any location, user, or device identifiers) may be kept strictly for model accuracy improvement and disease outbreak monitoring.
- **Legal Compliance:** Basic transaction logs or communication records may be temporarily held if required by applicable statutory laws or regulatory obligations.

---

## 5. Retention & Deletion Timeline
- **Processing Time:** Once verified, all identifiable personal data and linked records are permanently purged within **30 calendar days** of the request.
- **Backup Cycles:** Residual copies stored in automated disaster-recovery backups are routinely overwritten and permanently purged within standard backup retention cycles (not exceeding 60 days).
