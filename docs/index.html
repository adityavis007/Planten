Planten - Account and Data Deletion

App: Planten (AI Crop Doctor) • Developer: Aditya Vishwakarma • Google Play Compliant

1. Overview

At Planten, we respect user privacy and adhere strictly to Google Play's Account Deletion Policy. We provide all registered users with complete transparency and direct control over their personal profile and cloud-stored agronomic data.

2. How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can request account deletion at any time, even if you have already uninstalled the Planten app from your device.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Send an email to: adityavishakarma4@gmail.com
  2. Subject line: Planten - Account Deletion Request
  3. Include the following details in your email body:
    • Your Full Name (used in the app)
    • Your Registered Email Address (Firebase or Google Sign-In)
    • (Optional) Reason for account deletion

Our support team will verify and initiate the deletion process within 48 hours of receiving your request.

3. Data That Will Be Permanently Deleted

Upon verification, the following data is permanently purged:

  • Authentication Details: Firebase user authentication record, UID, and OAuth tokens.
  • Farmer Profile: Name, village, district, state, and primary crop selections.
  • Scan & Health History: Crop diagnosis records, symptoms entered, and sync timestamps.
  • Cloud Images: Cloud-backed leaf photos (if opt-in photo backup was enabled).

4. Data Retention and Timeline

Timeline: All active user profile data and diagnostic records are permanently deleted within 30 days of confirmation.

Retained Data: No personal identifiable information is kept. Only aggregated, fully anonymized crop disease metrics (with zero user or location identifiers) may be retained for regional disease forecasting.
